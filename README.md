# Epic Origin - User Guide

## What is this game?

Epic Origin is a turn-based strategy game combining exploration, management, and combat, inspired by Heroes of Might and Magic III.

On a fantasy continent, three races — Human, Heaven, and Ghost — each hold their own stronghold, vying for resources and territory. You play as the hero of one race, exploring unknown locations on the map, gathering resources, upgrading your stronghold, summoning units, and ultimately eliminating all enemy forces to become the sole ruler of the continent.

### Core Experience

- **Conquest**: From defending a single stronghold to ruling the entire continent — experience the thrill of growing from weak to powerful
- **Strategy**: Turn-based card battles where unit composition and racial counter-relationships let you outsmart stronger opponents
- **Exploration**: Resource nodes and random events bring surprises — unlock high-level units and earn valuable resources

---

## Quick Start

1. Launch the game and enter the main menu
2. Click **New Game**
3. Choose your race (Human / Heaven / Ghost)
4. Enter the map and begin your journey!

---

## Main Menu

The first screen you see after launching the game:

| Button | Function |
|--------|----------|
| New Game | Enter race selection and start a new game |
| Load Game | Enter save selection screen to load a previous save (button is disabled if no saves exist) |
| Settings | Open settings panel to adjust volume and AI difficulty |
| Exit Game | Close the game |

---

## Race Selection

Click on a race area on the map to view its details and backstory. Once you've decided, click **Confirm** to start the game.

Each race has its own strengths:
- **Human**: Balanced offense and defense, beginner-friendly
- **Heaven**: High HP, excels in prolonged battles
- **Ghost**: High attack power, favors quick strikes

---

## Game Interface

After entering the game, the top of the screen displays current status:

| Info | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Turn | Current turn number |
| Status | Shows "Your Turn", "Acted", or "AI Turn" |
| Resources | Current gold, building materials, and stronghold level |

Two buttons at the bottom of the screen:
- **End Turn**: Click after taking your action to pass the turn to AI
- **Save**: Open the save panel for manual saving

---

## Map Controls

### Moving Your Hero
- **Left-click** on a map tile to move your hero there
- You can only move once per turn, up to 3 tiles (Manhattan distance)
- Moving onto different tile types triggers corresponding events

### Actions That Don't Cost a Turn
- **Click your hero's current tile**: Triggers the tile event without consuming your action
- **Enter your own stronghold**: Opens the stronghold management panel without consuming your action

### Tile Effects

Stepping onto different tile types triggers different effects:

| Tile | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Resource | Randomly gain gold (3-8) or building materials (2-5) |
| Army Camp | Battle a random enemy force; win to gain cards or gold, lose to lose participating cards |
| Random Event | 40% gain resources / 30% lose gold / 30% nothing happens |
| Your Stronghold (green) | Enter stronghold management panel |
| Enemy Stronghold (purple) | Trigger a decisive battle |
| Obstacle | Impassable |

---

## Stronghold Management

Step onto your stronghold and click to open the management panel. All actions here are free (they don't consume your turn action):

### Upgrade Stronghold
- Spend building materials to increase stronghold level (max level 5)
- Higher levels unlock stronger units and increase per-turn resource production

### Summon Units
- Spend gold to summon unit cards into your deck
- You can only summon units whose level doesn't exceed your stronghold level
- Each card has a quantity selector to adjust how many to summon
- Cards appear grayed out and unclickable when you lack resources or stronghold level

### Leave
- Click the leave button to close the panel and return to the map

---

## Battle System

### Entering Battle
You automatically enter the battle scene when stepping onto an army camp or enemy stronghold.

### Battle Interface
- Your cards are displayed at the bottom, enemy cards at the top
- Selected cards float upward with a gold highlight

### How to Fight
1. On your turn, **click one of your cards** to select it
2. Click the **Confirm Attack** button to launch the attack
3. Both cards play through a full animation sequence: flip, confrontation, resolution
4. On the enemy's turn, AI automatically selects a card
5. Players alternate turns until one side is eliminated

### Racial Counters
Human > Heaven > Ghost > Human. The counter-attacking race deals 20% more damage; the countered race deals 20% less.

### flee
You can click the flee button to exit a battle, but you'll lose 50% of your cards.

### After Battle
After the battle ends, you automatically return to the map and continue the current turn.

---

## Game Menu

Press **ESC** during gameplay to open the game menu with 6 options:

| Option | Function |
|--------|----------|
| Continue | Close the menu and return to the game |
| Hero Status | View current gold, materials, stronghold level, total deck power, and all card details |
| Save | Open the save panel to save to one of 3 manual slots (existing saves will be overwritten) |
| Load | Display 3 save slots; select one to load (resets current progress) |
| Settings | Open settings panel to adjust volume and AI difficulty |
| Exit Game | Show a confirmation prompt; confirm to return to the main menu |

Press **ESC** to close sub-panels layer by layer (load/confirm exit first, then the menu).

---

## Save System

### Auto Save
The game automatically saves at the end of each turn. You can load the auto save from the main menu via **Load Game**.

### Manual Save
1. Press ESC during gameplay → click **Save**, or click the **Save** button at the bottom of the screen
2. 3 save slots are displayed, each showing existing save info (turn number, player, save time)
3. Click a slot to save; existing saves will be overwritten
4. A success message appears and automatically disappears after 3 seconds

### Load Save
- **Main Menu** → Load Game → select auto save or a manual save
- **In Game** → ESC → Load → select a save slot to load

---

## Settings Panel

### Volume
Drag the slider to adjust the overall game volume (0%-100%). Changes take effect immediately.

### AI Difficulty
Click the button to toggle between two difficulty levels. Changes take effect immediately:
- **Easy**: AI moves randomly, acts passively, upgrades and summons less frequently
- **Hard**: AI actively paths to resource nodes, aggressively upgrades its stronghold and summons high-level units

---

## Winning the Game

Defeat the enemy stronghold in battle to win. There is no turn limit — develop your forces at your own pace before launching your assault.



# 史诗起点 - 使用说明

## 这是一款什么游戏？

《史诗起点》是一款融合「探索-经营-战斗」的回合制策略游戏，灵感源自《英雄无敌3》。

在一片奇幻大陆上，人族、天堂族、鬼族三大种族各据一方，为争夺资源与领土展开征伐。你将扮演其中一个种族的英雄，在地图上探索未知点位、积累资源、升级据点、召唤兵种，最终剿灭所有敌对势力，成为大陆唯一的统治种族。

### 核心体验

- **征服**：从据点防守到全大陆统治，体验从小到大、由弱变强的成长跃迁
- **博弈**：回合制卡牌战斗中，利用兵种搭配与种族克制，以策略制胜
- **探索**：地图资源点、随机事件带来未知惊喜，解锁高等级兵种、获取大额资源

---

## 快速开始

1. 启动游戏后进入主菜单
2. 点击 **开始新游戏**
3. 选择你的种族（人族 / 天族 / 鬼族）
4. 进入地图，开始你的征程！

---

## 主菜单

启动游戏后首先看到主菜单界面，提供以下选项：

| 按钮 | 功能 |
|------|------|
| 开始新游戏 | 进入种族选择界面，开始一局新游戏 |
| 载入存档 | 进入存档选择界面，加载之前的存档（没有存档时按钮不可点击） |
| 设置 | 打开设置面板，调节音量和AI难度 |
| 退出游戏 | 关闭游戏程序 |

---

## 种族选择

点击地图上的种族区域查看该种族的详情和背景故事，确认选择后点击 **确认** 开始游戏。

三大种族各有特色：
- **人族**：攻防均衡，适合新手
- **天族**：高生命值，擅长持久战
- **鬼族**：高攻击力，速战速决

---

## 游戏界面

进入游戏后，屏幕上方显示当前状态信息：

| 信息 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 回合数 | 当前是第几回合 |
| 状态 | 显示"你的回合"、"已行动"或"AI回合" |
| 资源 | 当前金币、建材数量和据点等级 |

屏幕下方有两个按钮：
- **结束回合**：执行完行动后点击，轮到AI
- **存档**：打开存档面板手动保存

---

## 地图操作

### 移动英雄
- 鼠标左键 **点击地图格子**，英雄会移动到该位置
- 每回合只能移动一次，移动距离不超过3格
- 移动到不同类型的地格会触发对应事件

### 不消耗行动的操作
- **点击英雄当前所在格子**：触发格子事件但不消耗回合行动
- **进入己方据点**：打开据点管理面板，不消耗回合行动

### 地格效果

走到不同类型的地格上会触发对应效果：

| 地格 | 效果 |
|------|------|
| 资源点 | 随机获得金币（3-8）或建材（2-5） |
| 兵营 | 与随机敌军战斗，胜利可获得卡牌或金币，失败损失参战卡牌 |
| 随机事件 | 40%获得资源 / 30%损失金币 / 30%无事发生 |
| 己方据点（绿色） | 进入据点管理面板 |
| 敌方据点（紫色） | 触发决战 |
| 障碍物 | 不可通行 |

---

## 据点管理

走到己方据点上点击进入管理面板，可执行以下操作（均不消耗回合行动）：

### 升级据点
- 消耗建材提升据点等级（最高5级）
- 等级越高，可召唤的兵种越强，每回合资源产出越多

### 召唤兵种
- 消耗金币召唤兵种卡牌加入卡组
- 只能召唤等级不超过据点等级的兵种
- 每张卡牌旁有数量选择器，可调整召唤数量
- 资源不足或据点等级不够时，对应卡牌会显示为灰色不可点击

### 离开
- 点击离开按钮关闭面板，返回地图

---

## 战斗系统

### 进入战斗
走到兵营或敌方据点时自动进入战斗场景。

### 战斗界面
- 屏幕下方显示你的卡牌，上方显示敌方卡牌
- 选中卡牌会上浮并显示金色高亮

### 操作步骤
1. 你的回合时，**点击一张己方卡牌**选中
2. 点击 **确认出战** 按钮，发起攻击
3. 双方卡牌会进行翻牌、对峙、结算的完整动画
4. 敌方回合由AI自动选择出牌
5. 双方交替出牌直到一方全灭

### 种族克制
人族 > 天族 > 鬼族 > 人族，克制方伤害提升20%，被克制方伤害降低20%。

### 逃跑
战斗中可点击逃跑按钮退出，但会有半数卡牌损失。

### 战斗结束后
战斗结束后自动返回地图，继续当前回合。

---

## 游戏菜单

游戏中按 **ESC** 键打开游戏菜单，提供以下6个选项：

| 选项 | 功能 |
|------|------|
| 继续游戏 | 关闭菜单，返回游戏 |
| 英雄状态 | 查看当前金币、建材、据点等级、卡组总战力和所有卡牌详情 |
| 存档 | 打开存档面板，保存到3个手动槽位之一（已有存档会被覆盖） |
| 读档 | 显示3个存档槽位，选择一个加载（会重置当前进度） |
| 设置 | 打开设置面板，调节音量和AI难度 |
| 退出游戏 | 弹出确认提示，确认后返回主菜单 |

按 **ESC** 可逐层关闭子面板（先关读档/确认退出，再关菜单）。

---

## 存档系统

### 自动存档
每回合结束自动保存，无需手动操作。下次启动游戏可从主菜单 **载入存档** 读取自动档。

### 手动存档
1. 游戏中按 ESC → 点击 **存档**，或点击屏幕下方的 **存档** 按钮
2. 显示3个存档槽位，每个槽位显示已有存档的信息（回合数、玩家、保存时间）
3. 点击一个槽位保存，已有存档会被覆盖
4. 保存成功后显示提示信息，3秒后自动消失

### 读档
- **主菜单** → 载入存档 → 选择自动档或手动存档
- **游戏中** → ESC → 读档 → 选择存档槽位加载

---

## 设置面板

### 音量
拖动滑块调节游戏总音量（0%-100%），实时生效。

### AI难度
点击按钮在两个难度之间切换，立即生效：
- **简单**：AI随机移动，行动较被动，升级和召唤频率较低
- **困难**：AI主动寻路资源点，积极升级据点和召唤高等级兵种

---

## 胜负条件

攻克敌方据点且战斗胜利即赢得游戏。游戏没有回合上限，你可以按自己的节奏发展壮大后再发起进攻。
